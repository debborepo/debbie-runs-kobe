💡 WHY THIS TIMING WORKS
45–60 min between eating and running prevents acid reflux while giving your body fuel to burn. Evening running (after 7:30 PM) keeps UV exposure to zero. Brown rice's low glycemic index means glucose releases gradually — your muscles absorb it as fuel during the run, naturally flattening the insulin spike.
SUNDAY · 3 MIN HANDS-ON · RICE COOKER DOES THE REST
GINGER RICE + BOK CHOY BASE
Set it and forget it. Rice cooker runs unattended while you do anything else. Ginger in the water prevents reflux during the run. Makes 5 portions — this is the only cooking you do for the whole week.
RICE (2 min)
  • Rinse 2 cups brown rice, add to cooker
  • Drop in 2–3 slices of fresh ginger — done, press start
VEGGIES (1 min to prep, 5 min steam)
  • Halve a bag of baby bok choy, throw in the rice cooker steam tray above the rice
  • Cooks at the same time — zero extra pots
  • Drizzle sesame oil + sesame seeds when done
WEEKNIGHT USE
  • Grab a container from the fridge, microwave 2–3 min — meal done
  • Add soy sauce + sesame oil at the table to keep it feeling fresh each night
SUNDAY · 5 MIN HANDS-ON · OVEN DOES THE REST
SOY-SESAME CHICKEN BREAST
5 min to season, 25 min the oven does everything. You walk away. Lean chicken avoids the fat that triggers reflux — and soy + sesame is a Taiwanese pantry flavour you already know.
DO THIS WHILE RICE COOKS
  1. Preheat oven to 400°F / 200°C
  2. Put 1.5–2 lbs chicken breasts in a baking dish
  3. Pour over: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, pinch white pepper
  4. Put in oven. Set timer 25 min. Walk away
  5. Cool, slice, divide into 5 containers with rice + veg
FLAVOUR SWITCH (no extra time)
  • Splash of any dry cooking wine or apple cider vinegar = deeper flavour
  • Pinch of mixed spice or cinnamon = warmth without specialist spices
SUNDAY · 5 MIN HANDS-ON · OR WEEKNIGHT 8-MIN PAN
SOY-GINGER TOFU
Tofu is alkaline — it actively calms acid reflux. Two ways to cook it: batch in the oven Sunday (hands-off), or quick-pan on a weeknight if you want something fresh and hot in under 10 min.
SUNDAY BATCH (5 min hands-on, oven does rest)
  1. Cube 2 blocks extra-firm tofu — no pressing needed if you buy firm enough
  2. Toss with: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp grated ginger
  3. Spread on a tray, bake 400°F / 200°C for 25 min alongside the chicken
WEEKNIGHT PAN (8 min, all hands-on)
  1. Slice tofu into planks, pat dry with paper towel
  2. Pan on medium-high, tiny splash of sesame oil
  3. 3 min each side until golden — pour soy + ginger over, 1 min more
WEEKNIGHT · 10 MIN TOTAL · NO OVEN
STEAMED WHITE FISH
Fastest protein of the week. White fish digests in 30 min — you can eat later than usual and still run comfortably. Zero prep beyond placing it on a plate.
METHOD (10 min start to eat)
  1. Boil water in a pot or wok with a steamer rack
  2. Place fish fillet on a heatproof plate
  3. Lay ginger strips + scallion on top, drizzle 1 tbsp soy sauce + tiny sesame oil
  4. Steam 7–9 min — done when it flakes with a chopstick
  5. Microwave a rice portion from the batch while fish steams
OPTIONAL FLAVOUR (30 sec)
  • A few drops of white wine or lemon juice before steaming removes fishiness
  • Any white fish works — cod, tilapia, haddock, sole, whatever's fresh locally
WEEKNIGHT · 12 MIN · USES LEFTOVER RICE
QUICK CONGEE (粥)
The fastest hot meal on a tired night. Liquid base digests even quicker than solid rice — eat at 7:00 PM and run at 7:30 PM comfortably. Uses your Sunday batch rice so there's nothing new to cook.
METHOD (12 min)
  1. Add 1 portion leftover rice + 2 cups stock to a small pot
  2. Bring to boil, simmer 10 min — stir occasionally, it breaks down fast
  3. Stir in any shredded chicken or tofu from your batch containers
  4. Top with green onion + white pepper — done
WHY THIS BEATS SOLID RICE ON TIRED NIGHTS
  • Faster digestion = shorter wait before your run
  • Warming + comforting — feels like a real cooked meal
WEEKNIGHT · 12 MIN · HANDS-OFF ONCE STEAMING
TAIWANESE STEAMED EGG (蒸蛋)
2 min to mix, 10 min to steam unattended. Pure protein, zero fat, silky texture. Perfect on low-mileage nights when you want something lighter. Classic Taiwanese weeknight food.
METHOD
  1. Beat 3 eggs + 1.5 cups warm stock + 1 tsp soy sauce in a bowl
  2. Cover with a plate or cling wrap
  3. Steam on LOW for 10–12 min — do not let it boil hard or it goes bubbly
  4. Drizzle sesame oil + green onion to finish
SUNDAY · 2 MIN HANDS-ON · STEAM TRAY IN RICE COOKER
STEAMED SWEET POTATO (地瓜)
Drop them in the rice cooker steam tray while your rice cooks — zero extra effort. Lower GI than rice, naturally alkaline (great for reflux), and feels like a treat. Swap in whenever you want variety.
METHOD
  1. Wash 2–3 sweet potatoes, halve them — no peeling needed
  2. Place on the rice cooker steam tray above the rice
  3. Done when rice is done — takes the same time
WEEKNIGHT USE
  • Cold from the fridge — just microwave 90 sec
  • Mash one half with a fork, top with batch chicken or tofu — done
  • Cold sweet potato has even lower GI (resistant starch) — fine to eat straight from fridge
TRAVELLING · NO BATCH COOK · SUPERMARKET ANYWHERE
EUROPE TRAVEL MEALS
Every supermarket in Europe stocks these. No specialist ingredients, no rice cooker needed. All under 15 min. The only rule: eat 45–60 min before your run, keep it low-fat and low-acid.
OPTION 1 — PASTA + TUNA (10 MIN)
  • Boil pasta (any shape) — 8–10 min
  • Drain, top with a tin of tuna in spring water (not oil), drizzle of olive oil, black pepper
  • Why it works: pasta is a slow-release carb, tuna is lean fast-digesting protein, zero acid
OPTION 2 — EGGS + TOAST (8 MIN)
  • 2–3 scrambled or poached eggs on wholegrain toast
  • No butter on the toast — reflux trigger. Olive oil drizzle instead
  • Why it works: eggs digest quickly, wholegrain toast is low GI
OPTION 3 — RICE PACKET + CHICKEN (5 MIN)
  • Microwave rice packet (Uncle Ben's style — every European supermarket has these)
  • Top with rotisserie chicken from the deli counter — just pull the breast meat off
  • Drizzle soy sauce if you have it, or just black pepper + lemon
  • Why it works: zero cooking skill needed, same macros as your batch bowl at home
OPTION 4 — GREEK YOGHURT + OATS (3 MIN, NO HEAT)
  • Bowl of oats (soak in cold water 5 min if no microwave) + plain Greek yoghurt
  • Banana or berries on top
  • Best for: hotel rooms, no kitchen at all, morning long run days
  • Note: eat this 60 min before running — dairy needs a little longer to settle
WHAT TO KEEP IN YOUR BAG
  • Small bottle of soy sauce — makes any plain protein taste like home
  • Sesame oil sachet or small bottle — 1 tsp transforms a microwave rice packet
  • These fit in a carry-on, last the whole trip, available in Asian grocery shops across Europe
